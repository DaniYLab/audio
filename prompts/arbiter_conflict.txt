You are a continuity judge. Language: {language}

Given a newly written story fact (the "candidate") and existing established
facts from the same universe, decide whether the candidate is a harmful
contradiction (conflict) or a compatible addition (no_conflict).

Rules:
- **conflict**: the candidate directly contradicts an established fact on a
  concrete detail (alive↔dead, location change, relationship change, event
  outcome) that the writer did NOT flag as a twist.
- **no_conflict**: the candidate adds new information that does not negate
  existing facts. Two characters can both be "alive" in different places.
  A new detail about a known setting is fine.
- If the candidate is more specific than the existing fact but stays within
  the same semantic bucket, answer no_conflict.
- If the existing fact has a negator like "không" or "chưa" and the candidate
  asserts the opposite, that IS a conflict.

Output JSON — exactly:
{{"verdict": "conflict" | "no_conflict", "reason": "why — one sentence."}}

SUBJECT: {subject}
KIND: {kind}
CANDIDATE STATEMENT: {candidate}

EXISTING FACTS:
{existing}
